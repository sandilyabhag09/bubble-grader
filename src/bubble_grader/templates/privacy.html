Privacy policy

Bubble Grader · last updated {{ updated }}

What this app is

Bubble Grader is a grading tool used by teachers at a tutoring program. A teacher signs in with their Google account; the app reads the bubble-sheet scans that students turn in through Google Classroom, scores them against an answer key, and records the grades.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Classroom — the teacher's courses, class rosters (student names and email addresses), assignments, and student submissions. The app writes grades back to assignments it created and returns them to students.
  • Google Drive — read-only access to the scan files students attach to their Classroom submissions. The app does not read any other Drive files.
  • Gmail — sends score-report emails from the teacher's own address, only when the teacher clicks "Send feedback emails".
  • Account email — to identify the signed-in teacher and check they're on the program's teacher list.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Teachers' Google sign-in tokens, encrypted at rest, so the app can grade without the teacher being present. Deleted when the teacher revokes access.
  • Grades and per-question results for each submission, kept so teachers and students can look back at past tests.
  • Student scan files, kept only while a test is being graded and deleted afterwards (automatically after 14 days at the latest).

Data lives in a private database used only by this app. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than grading and reporting scores to the teacher and the student.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Bubble Grader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
